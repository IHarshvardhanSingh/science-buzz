Mussel Inspired Surgical Glue

Researchers have developed flexible adhesives that can stick to wet, moving tissue

Surgical adhesives must work in challenging conditions: living tissue is wet, flexible, and constantly moving. Researchers have developed bio-inspired adhesives designed to stick to wet tissues and remain flexible as they move. In a published study, a tough adhesive inspired by slug mucus sealed a defect in a pig’s heart—even while the heart was beating. The work could help inspire future surgical sealants, but it should not be described as a routine, approved treatment for human heart surgery.
